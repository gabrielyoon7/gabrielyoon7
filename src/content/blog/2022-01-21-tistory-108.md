> 이 글은 2022-01-21에 티스토리 블로그(leirbag.tistory.com/108)에 처음 작성한 글을 옮겨온 것입니다.

java.sql.SQLException: Expression #1 of SELECT list is not in GROUP BY clause and contains nonaggregated column '***\*\*\*\*\*\*\****' which is not functionally dependent on columns in GROUP BY clause; this is incompatible with sql\_mode=only\_full\_group\_by Query: SELECT \* FROM \`***\*\*\*\*\*\*\****\` GROUP BY \`***\*\*\*\*\*\*\****\` ORDER BY \`***\*\*\*\*\*\*\****\` Parameters: \[\]

MySQL 터미널에 다음과 같은 명령어를 입력해주면 된다.

<table><tbody><tr><td>SET&nbsp;GLOBAL&nbsp;sql_mode=(SELECT&nbsp;REPLACE(@@sql_mode,'ONLY_FULL_GROUP_BY',''));</td></tr></tbody></table>
